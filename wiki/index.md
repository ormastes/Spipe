# SPipe common wiki

This is the stable entrypoint for reusable SPipe knowledge. Research adapters
compose it with authorized company, organization, project, user, and host wiki
roots. Follow links to canonical `doc/` or `raw/` evidence; do not treat runtime
state as knowledge.

## Routes

- `../doc/00_llm_process/knowledge/` — documentation about the knowledge system.
- `../plugin/skills/spipe-research/SKILL.md` — scope-aware research procedure.

## Boundary

`runtime/` is derived and disposable. Private scope content is never copied into
this common wiki merely because it was mounted, retrieved, or frequently used.
